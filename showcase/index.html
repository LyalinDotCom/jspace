The premise

Depth behaves a little like time.

At each block, the residual stream can be projected into vocabulary space. Early readouts are often unstable. Later, task-relevant words can become legible and persist before output preparation.

This is not hidden chain-of-thought. It is a ranked vocabulary readout from a model activation. High rank is evidence worth inspecting—not proof of a causal workspace, consciousness, or a private sentence.

Two observations that survived inspection.

Both were run against the loaded checkpoint. The model response, layer checkpoints, grouped surface forms, positions, and ranks below are copied from the live instrument.
